# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want participation in the GetSorted project to be a respectful and constructive
experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback professionally.
- Focusing on the issue, code, or proposal rather than attacking people.
- Respecting different opinions and levels of experience.
- Sharing relevant information without exposing private or confidential data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, discrimination, personal
attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing someone else's private information,
and any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer
through the contact options available on the GitHub repository. Do not use public
Issues to share private or sensitive information.

The project maintainer will review reports and take appropriate action. Reports
will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including Issues, Pull Requests,
code reviews, and other project-related communication.
